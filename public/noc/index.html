Intranet NOC Diagnostics & Troubleshooting Matrix
  • ð¢ Why Intranet NOC Mode? Tests your direct LAN/SWAN fiber link to Patna HQ (10.X.X.0) with 0 commercial ISP overhead. Guarantees true internal portal, DPR submission & 1080p VC readiness metrics.
  • High Jitter (>15ms): Ruins video calls. To fix instantly, switch from Wi-Fi to a Cat6 wired Ethernet cable & check switch port duplex settings.
  • High Latency (>60ms): Optical link or routing delay. Check SFP optical RX/TX light levels (dBm) or clean fiber patch cords.
  • Low Throughput (<5Mbps): Bandwidth congestion. Verify switch interface QoS allocations and stop background backup streams.
  • Troubleshooting Isolation: If Intranet is fast but Internet is slow, the fault is with the external ISP gateway. If Intranet is slow, the fault is in local district cabling/fiber.
