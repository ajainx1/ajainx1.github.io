Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Cyber FreeRice application, we may collect the following information:

  • Authentication Data: If you choose to log in, we collect your phone number or email address to authenticate you securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage Data: We store your "Karmic Impact Score" (rice grains earned) locally on your device and associated with your account if logged in.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To save your progress and score across sessions.
  • To secure your account and prevent bot spam.

3. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your phone number or email is never shared with third-party advertisers. Authentication is handled securely by Google Firebase, and we do not store raw passwords.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@jumpstreet.systems.
