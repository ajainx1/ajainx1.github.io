Intranet NOC Diagnostics & Troubleshooting Matrix
  • 🏢 Why Intranet NOC Mode? Tests your direct LAN/SWAN fiber link to Patna HQ (10.133.22.8) with 0 commercial ISP overhead. Guarantees true internal portal, DPR submission & 1080p VC readiness metrics.
  • High Jitter (>15ms): Ruins video calls. To fix instantly, switch from Wi-Fi to a Cat6 wired Ethernet cable & check switch port duplex settings.
  • High Latency (>60ms): Optical link or routing delay. Check SFP optical RX/TX light levels (dBm) or clean fiber patch cords.
  • Low Throughput (<5Mbps): Bandwidth congestion. Verify switch interface QoS allocations and stop background backup streams.
  • Troubleshooting Isolation: If Intranet is fast but Internet is slow, the fault is with the external ISP gateway. If Intranet is slow, the fault is in local district cabling/fiber.

What is Activity XP & +150 Boost?

Bihar 38-District Real-Time Telemetry & Engagement Guide
⚡ Activity Experience Points (XP)
XP (Experience Points) measures real-time district operational engagement, network speed tests, diagnostic audits, and active link verification performed by DIOs, ADIOs, and FMS engineers across all 38 districts of Bihar.
1. Live 38-District Standings
Accumulated XP directly determines your district's ranking (#1 to #38). Districts with the highest verified activity earn the Gold 🥇, Silver 🥈, and Bronze 🥉 Podium spots on the State NOC Dashboard.
2. Level Up Progression
Every 500 XP unlocks a new district level (from Level 1 Cadet up to Level 10+ Enterprise NOC Master) and advances your operational tier from Operational to Elite Activity Tier.
3. What Does "+150 XP" Do?
Clicking +150 XP or executing a speed test logs a verified telemetry heartbeat for your district in the central NOC log, immediately awarding points and boosting your district's standing.
4. State NOC Operational Value
High-XP districts provide continuous diagnostic telemetry to the State NOC, ensuring optical fiber latency (ms) and core uptime (99.99%) are monitored with zero blind spots.
